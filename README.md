### About

Third-year Systems Engineering student at **Instituto Tecnológico de Zacatepec** (Mexico). I work on
applied computer vision — mostly gesture and sign-language recognition — and I spend most of my
free time on competitive programming and the mathematics that machine learning research runs on.

My long-term goal is research engineering: turning ideas from papers into systems that actually run.
Right now that means going deep on linear algebra, probability and optimization while shipping
projects that force me to learn things properly.

**Currently:** training for **ICPC México** · studying ML foundations · rewriting my sign-language
recognition pipeline in PyTorch
